# DMZ brand

The wordmark and the mark, rebuilt as vectors from the operator's logo (a 1254 px PNG on black). Everything here is
generated by `tools/brand/export.mjs`; edit the geometry there, never the files by hand.

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `logo.svg` | the wordmark, display cut, light letterforms: for dark grounds |
| `logo-on-light.svg` | the same, black letterforms: for light grounds |
| `logo-small.svg` | the small cut, for heights of 16–40 px (the app's header and rail carry it inline) |
| `mark.svg` | the compact mark: the M and its lime diagonal on a black tile with a cut corner. Favicon, avatars, app icons |
| `mark-{16,32,180,512,1024}.{png,webp}` | the mark rasterised (180 is the Apple touch icon, 512 and 1024 are store and PWA sizes) |
| `avatar.svg`, `avatar-1024.{png,webp}` | the social avatar: the wordmark on black, inside the circle networks crop to |

The PNG and the WebP of each size are the same pixels: Chromium rasterises the SVG and encodes both, the WebP
losslessly.

## Colours

Measured from the source as the median of every pixel whose 5×5 neighbourhood is one colour, so edges and noise
do not pull the value.

| Role | Hex | RGB | On black | On white |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ground | `#000000` | 0 0 0 | — | — |
| Letterforms | `#E8E6E4` | 232 230 228 | 16.9:1 | — |
| Lime, the M diagonal | `#C2FB55` | 194 251 85 | 17.2:1 | 1.2:1 |
| Grey, the Z diagonal | `#383D45` | 56 61 69 | 1.9:1 | 10.9:1 |
| Letterforms on light | `#000000` | 0 0 0 | — | 21:1 |

The grey is meant to be quiet: on black it sits between the ground and the letterforms, and the Z still reads from
its two bars and its cut. The app's tokens are close to these but not the same (`--fg` `#e9eae7`, `--acc` `#b4f03f`,
`--graphite` `#4b525a`); the mark keeps its own colours (`app/parts.css`, `/* ==== brand ==== */`).

## Construction

Units are the source's pixels, origin at the M's peak: the wordmark is 828 × 264, the cap height 248 (16–264), and
the M rises 16 above the cap. Each shape was measured by tracing its contour and fitting its edges. The source drew
one idea at several nearly-equal values (a D whose top chamfer is 41° and bottom 33°, stems from 53 to 60, bars of
48 and 52); the rebuild settles each family on one value:

- **one stroke**, t = 52, for every stem, bar and band, measured square to the edge;
- **one slope**, 4:5 (38.7°), for every diagonal in the D and the M; 45° for every cut in the Z;
- **one gap**, g = 20, for every cut inside a letter: the M's arm to the lime, the lime to the right stem, the Z's
  diagonal to its bottom bar;
- the D is symmetric about its middle, and its counter is its outline inset by t;
- the grey hangs from the Z's top bar, continues that bar's cut, and stops 14 short of the M, as in the source.

Letter spacing is the source's: D 0–214, M 238–543, Z 573–828.

**The small cut** opens g to 28. Below about 40 px tall a 20-unit cut is under two pixels on a 1x screen, and the
M's arm, lime and stem fuse into one shape; at 28 they stay three.

**The mark** is the M alone, redrawn on a 64-unit grid for 16 px: stroke 10, gap 5, every vertical edge on a
multiple of 4 so that at 16 px the stems land on whole pixels. The tile is the ground with the system's cut on its
top-right corner, so the mark reads on a light tab bar and a dark one without a second variant.

## Usage

- **Sizes.** `logo.svg` from 40 px tall up; `logo-small.svg` from 16 to 40 px; under 16 px, or in any square slot,
  the mark.
- **Clear space.** Keep at least t (a fifth of the letter height) clear on every side; for the mark, the tile is
  its own clear space.
- **Grounds.** `logo.svg` on black or a near-black surface; `logo-on-light.svg` on white or a pale neutral. Never
  on a mid-tone, a photograph or a gradient.
- **Colour.** The lime and the grey are never re-coloured and never swapped; the letterforms are `#E8E6E4` on dark
  and `#000000` on light, or `currentColor` where the page sets the ink. On white the lime is 1.2:1 and reads by its
  shape between the black letterforms, so it is never used alone on a light ground.
- **Form.** Never stretch, rotate, outline, round, shadow or glow the mark, and never set "DMZ" in a typeface in its
  place. Nothing is rounded; everything is cut (docs/DESIGN_SYSTEM.md).

## In the app

- `app/index.html` carries the small cut inline in the header and the desktop rail. Its letterforms are
  `currentColor` (the theme's `--fg`); its two diagonals take the classes `mk-l` and `mk-g`, coloured in
  `app/parts.css`. A light theme changes `--fg` and the wordmark follows; its diagonals stay the logo's own.
- The favicon is `mark.svg` as a `data:` URI in `<head>`, with `mark-32.png` as a `data:` URI for browsers that
  take no SVG icon. The CSP's `img-src 'self' data:` admits both, and no extra TELA document is needed.

## Regenerating

```
node tools/brand/export.mjs                           # the SVGs; no dependency
PW_DIR=<playwright> node tools/brand/export.mjs --raster   # also the PNG and WebP (tools/uitest/README.md)
node tools/brand/export.mjs --app                     # prints the index.html snippets
```
